# Launchr product page: BRIEF

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** Tobia: "Just make it extremely, extremely converting", "Keep it 3D. Keep it interactive as a website. Make sure that it works both from phone and from desktop." He also asked for "super value-driven, short, just like the other one that converts".

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** A motion studio's showreel on the site's paper. Calm frame, loud films. References: an Apple product page's device turn; a film festival reel; a record shop listening booth (you pick one up and hear it).
2. **Journey (authored).** The product working, first screen, with the buy button. Then the four looks, one at a time, each saying what Launchr did in that film. Then what it replaces. Then how easy it is. Then the last questions. Then buy.
3. **Energy curve.** Loud open (a real launch film playing on a device you can spin). Rising through the looks. Calm on "what it replaces" and the steps. Resolved and still at the close.
4. **Feeling, and the one moment.** Surprise ("Claude made that?"), then want ("I could have one for my product"), then relief ("no editor, no subscription"), then ease ("three steps"), then decision. The moment: the device turns and becomes a laptop playing a completely different launch film.
5. **What no other site does.** The device changes shape to match each video's format as you scroll: phone for vertical, laptop for landscape, a feed post for square. Real films, real soundtracks, one tap for sound.
6. **Distance from premium-minimal.** Premium-minimal frame (the site's Ink & Paper), with the energy carried by the films themselves.
7. **One world or scenes.** Distinct scenes. One pinned stage for the looks, then printed chapters.
8. **Assets.** Four finished example films from the product (VOLT energy drink, Ledgerly web app, Ledgerly mobile app, NOIR perfume), each with its own soundtrack. No generated assets needed.

## Feeling curve

- Act 1, hero (surprise): a real launch film already playing on a 3D phone, turning slowly; drag it and it follows your hand.
- Act 1, looks (want, rising): scrolling turns the device into the next format and the next film; each step names one concrete thing Launchr did.
- Act 2, what it replaces (relief, calm): three plain rows, the last one is €12 once.
- Act 3, ten minutes (ease): three steps and the conversation it has with you.
- Act 4, questions (trust).
- Close (decision, holds): price and button, still.

## The peak

"You scroll and the phone flips round into a laptop playing a whole different launch video." Act 1, the step into Tech dark.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where the phone turns into a laptop as you scroll, and every video on it was made by Claude in ten minutes.

## Rules kept

No em dashes, no invented numbers (no competitor prices), no scroll cue, one eyebrow, varied anchors, sound only on tap, reduced motion gets no pin and no WebGL, and the buy button is on the first screen.
