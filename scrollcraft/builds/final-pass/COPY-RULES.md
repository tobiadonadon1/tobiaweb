# Copy rules for this pass (researched 2026-08-28)

The operative subset. Full sourcing in the research transcript.

## The numbers that bite
- **111 words per VIEWPORT is the ceiling.** Users read ~20-28% of a page.
  Only at <=111 words do they read about half. (NN/g)
- **Removing hype alone = +27% measured usability.** Concise = +58%.
  Both plus scannable = +124%. (Morkes & Nielsen, 51 users)
- **Users perceive ~the first TWO WORDS** of a heading. 85% picked the right
  link from 11 characters. Never open with "Introducing", "Why", "How I". (NN/g)

## The rules
1. **Sub must add a NEW FACT.** Cover the headline with your hand: does the sub
   still tell you something? No -> delete it. (Shapiro)
2. **One idea per screen.** Cannot write the one sentence it survives on? Cut
   the section. (Masterson, Rule of One)
3. **Concrete language is JUDGED MORE TRUE** than abstract with identical
   content. (Hansen & Wanke 2010, replicated) Zombie nouns banned as subjects:
   range, context, vision, clarity, alignment, potential, capability.
4. **Specificity IS the credential** when you have no authority. A claim a
   competitor could also make is not a claim. (Heath bros, Harry Dry)
5. **Order the honesty: PROOF -> ADMISSION -> REFRAME.** Never admission alone.
   "No customers yet." on its own is a non-refutational two-sided message and
   it COSTS credibility. "No customers yet. Three are testing it and I am
   rewriting what they broke." buys it. (Ein-Gar/Shiv/Tormala JCR 2012; Eisend
   meta-analysis) NOTE: the blemishing effect needs LOW-effort processing; the
   project pages are high-effort reads, so the refutation is mandatory.
6. **Disqualify out loud.** Three lines of "this is not for you if". Only
   someone with standards turns work away. (Avis, AmEx, Jonathan Stark)
7. **Buttons: first person, name the object.** "Start MY free trial" beat
   "your" by 90%. "Request pricing" beat "Request a quote" by 161%.
8. **ONE form field.** 3 fields peaked at ~25% conversion; 4 fell sharply.
9. **Reassurance microcopy must be a POSITIVE FACT.** "100% privacy, we will
   never spam you" LOST 18.7%. "We guarantee 100% privacy. Your information
   will not be shared." GAINED 19.5%. Naming the fear plants it.
10. **Humility is not hedging.** Stating a limitation as fact = humility, keep.
    Softening a claim you are making anyway = hedging, kill. Hedges measure as
    less credible and less competent. (Erickson 1978)

## Delete on sight
paraphrase subtitles · body that restates the H2 above it · microcopy under a
field repeating the label · scene-setters ("In today's world") · rhetorical
question headings ("Why work with me?") · two equal CTAs in one screen · lists
over three · "Learn more" as link text · hedges (just, sort of, I think, a bit)
· unlock/unleash/empower/supercharge/seamless/leverage/transform/journey ·
adverb stacks · passive voice where the actor is Tobia · em dashes ·
superlatives · vague modesty ("some companies") · "coming soon" with no window

## The hard-truth headline pattern (for Superhuman's premise)
Line 1 = a fact the reader already accepts, free to agree with.
Line 2 = the turn. Must be FALSIFIABLE and CONCRETE.
Best candidates researched:
- "Everyone got the same tools. The gap got wider."   <- CHOSEN
- "Access stopped being the bottleneck. Almost nobody noticed."
- "The tool was never the hard part." / "Knowing what to ask it is."
